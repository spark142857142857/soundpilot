SoundPilot 0.1.2 — review instructions

Purpose: manage overlapping media volume across Chrome tabs. No account, payment, API key, or credentials are required by the extension.

1. Install the supplied package and allow access on the media websites being tested. Open the toolbar popup. Auto volume should be ON and Background volume should show 25%.
2. Open two tabs with ordinary HTML audio/video. For a public example, open https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ in two tabs. Complete any website consent prompt, ensure both players are unmuted, and start from a paused state with nonzero player volume.
3. Directly click Play in tab A, then directly click Play in tab B. A should remain playing at 25% of its original media volume. B remains at its original volume. The popup identifies B under Listening to and A as Lowered to 25%.
4. Pause B. A should return to its original volume after a short recovery delay without being restarted. B must remain paused.
5. Reverse the order: pause A, play B, then play A. B should now be lowered. This verifies the same rule works for music and video in either order.
6. With both playing, change Background volume to 50%. The background source should use half its original volume. Close the popup; automatic control continues.
7. Reopen the popup and choose Reset to 25%. Then turn Auto volume OFF. Controlled volumes should return to their original values.
8. Closing the focused tab should also restore the most recent source still playing. Simply switching between tabs must not change priority.

Playback intent uses recent direct page input. Automatic next tracks, ads, previews, or OS media keys may not behave like a direct Play click. Some sites display volume on a nonlinear slider, so a 25% ratio does not necessarily put their visible slider at 25%. The extension does not control detached audio or independent Web Audio graphs and does not auto-resume paused media.

Tab titles, site information, media state, and playback-related input timing are processed locally for this feature. No audio is captured or sent to a server. All extension code is bundled in the ZIP.

Turn Auto volume OFF before reloading/removing the extension. After an extension update, refresh existing media pages. This version fixes uncaught extension-context errors when old pages continue DOM/Shadow DOM work during extension reload.
